00 How this works

Each strategy runs forward on live data from the day it started, with no real money. It decides on a trading day's close and fills at the next day's open, paying commission at {{rate .Costs.CommissionRate}} × {{.Costs.BrokerDiscount}} (minimum NT${{ntd .Costs.MinCommission}}) and {{rate .Costs.SellTax}} sell tax ({{rate .Costs.ETFSellTax}} on ETFs), from radar.toml. Dividends are reinvested in the same stock. Values are what you would have after selling everything at the last close, fees and tax included. {{.Benchmark}} is the same starting money bought on the first fill day and held.
