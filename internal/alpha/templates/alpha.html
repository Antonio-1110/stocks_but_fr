How to read this
  • Every trading day, each signal ranks the liquid common stocks (20M NTD a day traded, price ≥ 10). We buy at the next open and sell at the open after Hold trading days. A buy at a limit-up open can't fill, so it doesn't count.
  • Net is the average trade's return above the average liquid stock, after one round trip of fees and tax. Score signals trade their top 10%; event signals trade every stock with the event.
  • t says how sure: around 2 can be luck when many ideas are tried. Passes needs t ≥ {{num .Hurdle}} in 2012–2019 and t ≥ 2 with a positive net from 2020, which nothing here was tuned on.
  • A day late is the 2020+ net when you act one day after the signal. If it drops to nothing, the edge is only there for whoever moves first.
  • Flows data starts in 2013, so flow signals have a shorter design period.
